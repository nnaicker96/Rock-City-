These are distinct developmental audiences. Do not give the youngest children adult content with shorter sentences or give older children toddler activities.

## Teaching purpose

Teach Transformation explicitly: drawing near to God, intentionally following Jesus, developing Christlike relationships and serving others. Then connect this foundation to October’s Next Door evangelism theme. There was no September Children’s Church programme, so the foundation cannot be assumed.

Each lesson needs a clear idea children can understand and a biblical story or passage that carries it. Activities should reinforce that idea rather than merely occupy the group. Use familiar South African settings and examples.

## Practical constraints

Delivery is in class, with minimal preparation and no dependence on devices. Precious Pearls activities cannot depend on independent reading. Use varied activities suited to the task: movement and role-play for no-print sessions; matching, colouring or simple crafts where printing/materials are allowed; more complex puzzles for older learners.

Use established children’s church resources as inspiration for structure and activity variety when researching. Do not reproduce protected resources indiscriminately or pass improvised low-quality pictures off as finished illustrations.

## Programme versus facilitator guide

The latest request calls for **two separate documents**: a programme and a facilitator guide. The programme contains the teaching flow, story content and age-appropriate substance. Teacher actions, transitions, prompts and activity setup belong in the guide.

Make both easy to scan. Avoid repetitive DO / ASK / SAY labels that make the programme harder to follow. Explain unfamiliar activities concretely without burying the teacher in unnecessary reading.
