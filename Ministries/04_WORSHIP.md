# Worship

## Formation context

Worship in the Transformation year emphasises proximity to God. Prayer, the Word, fasting and surrender are part of that relationship. Ministry preparation should arise from personal and shared engagement with the teaching.

The recorded rehearsal rhythm is Thursday, with Sunday services. Treat particular dates and service arrangements as live scheduling information.

## Planning Center

Worship scheduling belongs in Services. Earlier rollout planning estimated approximately 15–16 worship team members and called for **two vocal positions per plan**, with separate single-service and dual-service templates.
