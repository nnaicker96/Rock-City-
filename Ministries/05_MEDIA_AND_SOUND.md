# Media and Sound Production

## Organisational boundary

Media sits under Tech Rock; lighting sits under Media. **Sound is excluded from Tech Rock’s defined scope**, even though Nikyle personally works across sound and media. Keep organisational ownership separate from one person’s responsibilities.

## Recorded production environment

- Soundcraft SI Impact 32-channel console and stagebox.
- Four EV 12-inch front-of-house speakers; three EV 12-inch wedges.
- Hybrid handheld microphones; recorded count thirteen.
- Cajon with Rode microphone; bass via Samson DI; electric via BBS DI; Yamaha keys via Samson DI.
- Roland V-Drums sent to the SI Impact in stereo.
- Streaming workflow described as ProPresenter → Blackmagic → Facebook.
- Venue: canvas dome.

This is a recorded setup, not a current channel patch or equipment inventory verified on site.

## Operating constraint

Pastor Paul’s microphone is recorded as **never muted**. Preserve that explicit operating requirement when proposing a workflow. Do not use an automated generic mute routine that conflicts with it.

## Technical advice

Do not invent exact gain, compression, routing or RF settings from equipment names alone. Use current meter readings, screenshots and signal-path information. Explain the setting’s purpose and what to observe when adjusting it.

## Visual production

Slides, posters and livestream elements follow the Rock City brand system. Keep supplied photographs faithful and preserve specified aspect ratios, text and scripture translations.
