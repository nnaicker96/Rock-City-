Portable operating context • Prepared 3 October 2026 • Version 1.0

## What this is

This pack explains who Nikyle is, how he works, Rock City Church’s established context, and the constraints that should govern work produced for him. It is designed for Notion, AI project knowledge, a developer handover or a private Git repository.

Read the relevant pages before working. Do not assume uploading a pack means an AI has read every page or will remember it in future conversations. Explicitly point it to this page and the relevant topic pages.

## Start instruction for an AI or collaborator

Use this folder as operating context for my request. Read 00_START_HERE, 01_ABOUT_NIKYLE, 02_WORKING_PREFERENCES and 03_HARD_CONSTRAINTS, then the pages relevant to the task. Preserve established decisions, exact scripture references, approved wording and protected design elements. Distinguish established context, dated plans and new proposals. Do the requested work; do not stop at offering to do it. Do not claim to have read inaccessible files or completed unperformed actions. Ask only when a missing answer materially blocks the work.

## How to resolve conflicts

Follow Nikyle’s current explicit instruction first. Use current task source material and approved project decisions for that specific task. Apply the relevant standing theology, ministry and brand constraints together. Generic best practice fills gaps; it does not override those constraints.

A newer specific instruction supersedes an older instruction about the same thing. A preference for NIV on a particular slide does not change the KJV requirement for the Transformation leaders’ pack. If two applicable requirements still conflict, identify the conflict precisely rather than silently selecting one.
