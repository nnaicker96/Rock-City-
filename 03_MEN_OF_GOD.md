# Men of God

## Audience and emphasis

Adult men aged 18 and older. Draw Near connects to Transformation through saturation, intentional proximity, default drift, formation and intentional covenanting. The user wants an interactive session with substantive group discussion.

Real-world parallels may come from sport, engineering, technology or birds flying in V formation. Explain the connection precisely; do not let the example turn the session into productivity coaching.

## Preserve revisions accurately

An early three-movement proposal was “Man of God Draw Near,” “Man of God look ahead” and “Man of God, force multiplier.” Later feedback explicitly preserved **Stay Close** and changed movement two to **Man of God, Choose This Day**, using **Philippians 2:1–13**.

Do not present the early three-title list as the final approved outline. The exact final third movement and complete approved wording are not reproduced in this pack. Retrieve the final source before revising that session.

## Protected teaching decisions

Stay Close was approved and was not to be rewritten when feedback concerned the other movements. Choose This Day concerns discipleship and intentional covenanting; do not substitute self-help language or change its passage.
