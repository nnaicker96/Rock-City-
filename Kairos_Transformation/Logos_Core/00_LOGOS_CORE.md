# Logos Core — Kairos Transformation

## Place in the architecture

**Rock City Church → Kairos 2026–27: Transformation → Logos Core → LABs**

Logos Core is distinct from the Steering Committee and Deaconate. Its complete formal mandate, membership and reporting lines are not established in the current context, so do not invent governance authority.

Within the Transformation work, Logos Core functions as a theological and leadership formation space: receive the Word, wrestle with it, allow it to form the leaders first, establish shared theological language, and then cascade the substance into ministry.

## Governing posture

**Saturation before cascade.**

The existing Transformation engagement principles govern Logos Core:

- Saturate ourselves in the Word.
- Be a reservoir, not merely a channel.
- Alignment before function.
- Counsel that carries.
- Modelling beyond mimicking.
- People being formed, together.

A Logos Core session should not become a content factory where leaders immediately ask, “What can we preach or post?” The first question is what God is revealing and what that revelation is doing in us.

## LAB architecture

“LAB” is used as a working formation environment, not a rigid sermon template. Previous LAB work has been strongest when it moves through Scripture, theological tension, formation, discussion and ministry translation rather than lecture alone.

Use this core movement:

### 1. Revelation
What are we seeing of God and Christ in the text? What assumptions need to be corrected? What is the gospel reality beneath the theme?

### 2. Tension
Where does that revelation confront default drift, church culture, familiar behaviour or our existing posture?

### 3. Formation
What is God seeking to renew in mind, affection, desire, habit, identity and relationship?

### 4. Practice
What practices create space for faithful response without becoming mechanical formulas or legalism?

### 5. Function
What does the formed life now do? How does Christ become visible in witness, worship, obedience, service and neighbour-love?

### 6. Cascade
What must remain intact when the teaching is translated into sermons, Kids Rock, Youth, Men of God, City Life Groups, worship environments, media and other ministry settings?

## Theological guardrails

- Keep Christ and Scripture central.
- Transformation is not self-improvement.
- Activity is not proof of formation.
- Do not use guilt as the primary engine for obedience.
- Spiritual disciplines serve relationship; they do not replace it.
- Emotion may accompany encounter but is not the measure of God's nearness.
- Evangelism is not merely event invitation.
- Worship is not merely music or public expression.
- The five Rock City pillars remain the church framework; Logos Core must not invent a replacement framework.
- Revelation → Formation → Function is the governing Transformation movement.

## LAB outputs

A developed LAB can generate:
- theological thesis and tensions;
- primary and supporting Scripture map;
- leader formation questions;
- discussion movements;
- sermon-development territories;
- ministry cascade notes;
- creative and symbolic language;
- doctrinal/interpretive cautions;
- unresolved questions requiring further study.

These outputs are downstream of formation, not substitutes for it.
