## POPIA / Privacy Principle
Access must be responsibility-based and least-necessary.

## Access Rules
- individual accounts;
- no shared credentials;
- scoped access;
- controlled exports;
- periodic access review;
- escalation for incidents;
- avoid unnecessary duplication of personal data.

## High-Risk / Human Approval Areas
Human approval should remain in the loop for:
- permissions;
- person/household merges;
- safeguarding;
- finance;
- destructive changes;
- exceptional data corrections;
- broad exports.

## Children
Kids Rock data and attendance require safeguarding-aware design. Do not replace secure check-in processes with an unsecured QR/custom form simply to remove friction.

## Repositories
Never commit:
- passwords;
- API keys;
- access tokens;
- private credentials;
- sensitive exports;
- unnecessary personal information.

## Operational Principle
Convenience does not override privacy, safeguarding or access control.
