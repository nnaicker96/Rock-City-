# Ministry + Transformation Context

## 2026–27 Kairos Theme
**Year theme:** TRANSFORMATION

### Central Conviction
God is constant; people must change. Transformation requires intentionality rather than passive drift.

### Four Core Areas
1. **Worship — Proximity**
   - proximity over petitioning;
   - outcomes: Alignment, Clarity, Power, Unity;
   - drift can come through temptations, trials, priorities and individual pursuits.

2. **Discipleship — Covenanting**
   - covenanting is an entry into intentional pursuit;
   - movement is opt-in rather than passive;
   - cascade concept: `1 → 3 → 12 → 30 → 300`;
   - includes Spiritual Vitality, the “three chairs,” Growth Mapping and the Transformation Zone.

3. **Fellowship — Christ-like Culture**
   - community must embody the “why,” not merely run mechanical check-ins;
   - people are formed in relationship and shared culture.

4. **Ministry — Push to Community Change**
   - transformation must move outward into kingdom/community impact.

## Transformation Visual Metaphor
A recurring visual language uses:
- caterpillar;
- cocoon;
- butterfly;
- malformed “butterpillar” where conceptually appropriate.

Established visual details:
- caterpillar may use red stripes;
- cocoon triad: Gold `#E8A93B`, Green `#3FA876`, Blue `#3093BD`;
- butterfly wings can carry all five pillar colours.

These are thematic assets, not mandatory decorations on every Rock City design.
