# Non-Negotiables + Failure Modes

## Never Do These
1. Do not invent requirements when established context exists.
2. Do not change approved copy, scripture, dates, names or visual elements during an unrelated revision.
3. Do not add generic slogans.
4. Do not add generic crests/shields.
5. Do not add sepia haze.
6. Do not reassign the five pillar colours.
7. Do not use Cape Town/Table Mountain when Johannesburg context is required.
8. Do not invent streets/buildings when real Rock City references have been supplied.
9. Do not regenerate identifiable people in ways that alter likeness.
10. Do not make children’s material text-heavy, especially for ages 4–7.
11. Do not let facilitator guides diverge from worksheets.
12. Do not present generic self-help as biblical ministry formation.
13. Do not conflate Tech Rock with Sound.
14. Do not put sound imagery into Tech Rock identity by default.
15. Do not treat Lighting as Sound; it belongs under Media.
16. Do not bypass Planning Center safeguarding/permissions with custom tooling.
17. Do not grant broad backend access when Church Center is sufficient.
18. Do not create a disconnected task system with no accountable system location.
19. Do not claim an integration/deployment/edit succeeded unless verified.
20. Do not commit credentials or sensitive personal data to repositories.

## Revision Discipline
If the instruction says:
- “change X” → change X.
- “keep Y” → preserve Y.
- “do not change anything else” → all other elements are locked.

## When Context Conflicts
Use this order:
1. current explicit user instruction;
2. latest explicit correction;
3. current approved project brief;
4. stable context pack;
5. older project history;
6. generic best practice.

Security, legal, privacy and safeguarding requirements remain mandatory.

## Quality Test Before Delivery
Ask:
- Is this recognisably Rock City?
- Did I preserve the exact brief?
- Did I accidentally invent anything?
- Is the theology/ministry framing faithful to the established context?
- Is it usable in the actual medium?
- Are system ownership and permissions correct?
- Is any temporary detail being mistaken for a permanent rule?
