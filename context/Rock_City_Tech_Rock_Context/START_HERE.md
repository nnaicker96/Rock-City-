## Core Operating Model
Rock City Church is the ministry organisation. Planning Center is the primary ministry system of record and Church Center is the normal member-facing layer. Tech Rock is responsible for digital infrastructure, IT, systems, Planning Center administration/enablement, and Media. Sound is a separate production function and is not part of Tech Rock. Lighting belongs under Media.

The working ecosystem may include:
- Planning Center — people, ministry structures, service planning, registrations, attendance/check-ins, publishing.
- Google Drive — documents, files, assets and shared ministry folders.
- Notion / church operations layer — planning, projects, actions, requests, meetings, decisions, SOPs and knowledge where adopted.
- GitHub — version-controlled code, context packs, web projects and technical documentation.
- Custom web/apps — experience or operations layers, never a replacement for safeguarding or authoritative ministry records.
