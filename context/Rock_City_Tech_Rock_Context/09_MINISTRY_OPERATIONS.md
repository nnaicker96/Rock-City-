## Kids Rock
Age groups:
- **Precious Pearls:** 4–7 — Green `#3FA876`
- **Super Sapphires:** 8–10 — Blue `#3093BD`
- **Dynamo Diamonds:** 11–13 — Red `#D63D3D`

### Curriculum Rules
- age appropriateness is non-negotiable;
- Pearls must not receive reading-heavy worksheets;
- Pearls: pictures, matching, colouring, simple crafts, role-play;
- Sapphires: puzzles, word searches, sequencing, scenario choices;
- Diamonds: short readings, crosswords, reflective prompts, debates;
- facilitator guides must correspond exactly to worksheets;
- separate facilitator instructions from child activity material;
- draw on credible children’s ministry/education patterns rather than inventing filler;
- low-prep classroom delivery is preferred;
- South African examples are useful;
- no device dependency.

## Youth
Preferred youth material is discussion-led and reflective rather than contrived games.
Transformation anchors have included:
- caterpillar → cocoon → butterfly;
- Romans 12:1–2;
- Romans 8:29;
- intentional formation and transformation.

## Men of God
Transformation work has used strong biblical/theological framing, practical real-world parallels and round-table discussion rather than generic masculinity/self-help language.

Concepts used include:
- Draw Near;
- intentional proximity;
- default drift;
- saturation;
- formation;
- covenanting;
- force multiplier;
- real-world parallels from sport, technology, engineering and formation systems.

## Logos Core
Treat Logos Core as part of Rock City’s strategic/theological formation context, not merely a generic content group. When describing it, use the established purpose from the relevant project rather than inventing a vague ministry description.

## Steering Committee / Leadership Work
Leadership artifacts should be concise, usable, well-designed and operationally clear. Where decisions/actions are generated, they should have accountable owners and a real system location.
