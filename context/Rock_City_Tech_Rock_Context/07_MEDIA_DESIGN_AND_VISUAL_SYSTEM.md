# Media, Design + Visual System

## Core Visual Character
Rock City design should feel:
- contemporary;
- editorial;
- concept-led;
- deliberate;
- ministry-specific;
- visually strong without clutter.

It should not look like a generic church template or generic AI art.

## Typography
**Display/headings:** Bebas Neue  
**Body:** clean sans-serif

Use Bebas Neue deliberately for strong display hierarchy, not as an excuse to make every line oversized.

## Lock-Up
Preserve:
`ROCK | CITY | CHURCH`

Do not casually redraw, bend, distort, replace or invent alternate lock-ups.

## Pillar Colours
- Evangelism — `#D63D3D`
- Worship — `#E8A93B`
- Discipleship — `#3093BD`
- Fellowship — `#3FA876`
- Ministry — `#7B4DA0`

Do not reassign meanings.

## Hard Visual Constraints
Avoid:
- sepia haze;
- generic crests/shields;
- fake glossy marks;
- generic church stock imagery;
- excessive gradients;
- glassmorphism;
- decorative blobs with no concept;
- clip-art;
- clutter;
- unnecessary icons;
- generic “three phrase” slogans;
- invented extra typography/copy;
- cheap or visibly inconsistent AI illustration.

## Photography + Likeness
When supplied photographs contain identifiable people:
- preserve likeness;
- do not casually regenerate faces;
- do not alter identity, age or facial structure;
- use the supplied photo directly where possible if likeness preservation is critical.

## Johannesburg Context
When a brief is geographically grounded:
- use Johannesburg context;
- do not substitute Cape Town/Table Mountain;
- do not invent streets or surroundings when real references were supplied;
- South African people should look credibly diverse and locally contextualised.

## Projection in the Dome
For presentation/projection work:
- favour lighter images/backgrounds where requested;
- maintain strong contrast;
- make typography bold and readable at distance;
- avoid muddy dark detail;
- scale to standard presentation/Canva slide proportions when requested;
- do not overload slides.

## Posters
Poster hierarchy should be intentional:
1. main concept/title;
2. key supporting message;
3. essential date/time/location;
4. scripture or campaign marker where specified;
5. Rock City identity.

Do not add filler.

## Presentations
- use a coherent visual system across slides;
- keep text large and scannable;
- one conceptual job per slide where possible;
- scripture slides must preserve exact requested translation/text;
- light projection-friendly treatments are often preferable in the Dome.

## Documents
Operational documents are still Rock City artifacts.
They should not default to generic Word-document styling.
Use:
- Rock City hierarchy;
- pillar colour logic where relevant;
- Bebas Neue-led headings;
- editorial spacing;
- clean tables/callouts;
- deliberate page architecture.

## Campaign-Specific Visual Language
The Transformation year can use caterpillar/cocoon/butterfly imagery, but this is a thematic language, not a mandatory logo.

### Next Door / Evangelism
Established direction has included:
- John 1:14;
- neighbourhood presence;
- Rock City Dome and actual surroundings;
- diverse South African people;
- integrated visual treatment;
- no sepia;
- five-colour butterfly where appropriate.

## Tech Rock Visuals
Tech Rock should feel part of Rock City, not like a generic SaaS company. Do not reach for generic computers, circuit boards, gears, clouds, sound equipment or fake futuristic symbols.

## Revision Rule
When instructed to change specific elements and “DO NOT CHANGE ANYTHING ELSE,” preserve every non-target element.
