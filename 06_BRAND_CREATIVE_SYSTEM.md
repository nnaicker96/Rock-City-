# Rock City Brand and Creative System

## Core identity

Lockup: **ROCK | CITY | CHURCH**. Display typeface: **Bebas Neue**. Body type: clean, readable sans-serif. Use hierarchy and deliberate spacing; long paragraphs should not be set in display type.

## Transformation visual language

Contemporary editorial composition, meaningful imagery, spatial depth, layered organic forms, controlled colour, sophisticated texture and negative space. Transformation hero graphics use non-white backgrounds. Atmosphere must not become sepia haze.

Established motifs include a caterpillar on a leaf, an upside-down caterpillar on a tree, a sealed cocoon, a cocoon further along, a five-colour butterfly and the malformed “butterpillar.” The caterpillar has red stripes. The cocoon triad uses gold #E8A93B, green #3FA876 and blue #3093BD. Tree compositions and triptychs exist within the visual vocabulary.

Treat these as established motifs, not a requirement to add every motif to every design. Obtain the actual reference artwork for a faithful revision.

## Hard exclusions

No sepia haze. No generic church crests or shields. No unnecessary icons, invented slogans, generic clip-art or cheap stock-template styling. Do not add decorative wording to fill empty space. Keep typography straight, readable and correctly spelled.

## Photographs and people

Preserve supplied people’s likeness. Do not regenerate, beautify, reinterpret or replace a person when the photograph must remain faithful. Crop, mask and layer the original when appropriate. If available tools cannot preserve the photograph faithfully, explain that limitation rather than claim an altered version is unchanged.

## Deliverable rules

Posters need a clear information hierarchy and the exact supplied event details. Presentations need legible type at viewing distance and the requested aspect ratio. Internal guides and operational documents must still feel like Rock City while keeping body copy easy to read.

For print, use the specified colour or black-and-white mode. Branding must not undermine legibility or ink economy. Markdown records the design rules but does not embed Bebas Neue or reproduce a finished poster layout.

## Next Door-specific treatment

Use the actual Rock City Dome and reference surroundings; do not invent streets. The requested campaign imagery is fully animated, with diverse South African people, visible dome branding and natural variation in the directions people face. Do not introduce photorealism into that brief. Exact campaign wording is on the Current Context page.
