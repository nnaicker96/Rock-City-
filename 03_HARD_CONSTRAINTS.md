## Task-specific rules that must not become universal

Next Door’s supplied dome scene must be animated and grounded in the actual surroundings. This does not ban photography in other projects. Transformation hero graphics use non-white backgrounds; a practical black-and-white printable guide can still use white paper. KJV applies to the leaders’ pack; a requested NIV slide stays NIV.

The register records user requirements. It is not a declaration of legal policy, a staff permissions list or an automatic instruction to change a live system.
